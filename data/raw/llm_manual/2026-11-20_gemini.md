2. Ethereum (ETH) — The DeFi & Layer-2 Settlement Bellwether

Why Watch: As the primary settlement layer for decentralized finance (DeFi), tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), and major Layer-2 rollups, Ethereum is typically the first large-cap asset to absorb liquidity when traders rotate down the risk curve from Bitcoin.

Key Metrics & Catalysts:
- ETH/BTC Ratio: Tracking whether ETH is outperforming or underperforming BTC tells you immediately if risk appetite is expanding.
