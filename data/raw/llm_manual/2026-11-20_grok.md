Here are 5 cryptocurrencies worth watching this week (as of November 20, 2026), ranked by a mix of market influence, near-term catalysts, relative resilience or volatility potential, and liquidity. The broader market is under pressure—Bitcoin near $61k–$62k with ongoing ETF outflows, extreme fear sentiment, and recent weakness across majors—so these are framed as monitoring candidates rather than buy recommendations.

1. Bitcoin (BTC)

The clear market leader and risk barometer. It has been testing support in the low-$60k area amid multi-week declines, heavy ETF outflows (hundreds of millions daily at points), and macro headwinds (Fed-related expectations). Options expiry and any shift in institutional flows or sentiment will set the tone for the entire space. Stabilization or a decisive move here is the single biggest near-term driver for alts.

2. Solana (SOL)

Showed relative strength during/around the recent Breakpoint conference in London (ecosystem updates, institutional/tokenization mentions including DTCC-related plans, and network milestones). Trading around the low-$70s with solid on-chain activity. Residual conference momentum, developer/ecosystem news, and any rotation into high-activity L1s make it one of the more interesting large-cap watches in a risk-off tape.

3. Hyperliquid (HYPE)

Standout for product fundamentals: dominant on-chain perpetual futures volume/revenue, token buybacks/burns, and expansion into traditional assets/stocks/commodities perps. Recent regulatory chatter (CFTC pathways for on-chain venues, Hong Kong listing notes) and ongoing platform activity keep it in focus even as the token has pulled back with the market (recently in the mid-$60s range). One of the cleaner "real usage + revenue" narratives amid the broader weakness.

4. LayerZero (ZRO)

Immediate supply catalyst: a notable token unlock (estimates in the ~23–31 million ZRO range, roughly $30–40M+ depending on price and tracker) around November 20, going to strategic partners, core contributors, and related allocations. This can drive short-term volatility (unlocks do not automatically equal selling, but they often increase attention and potential pressure). LayerZero also has upcoming infrastructure milestones (e.g., Zero mainnet-related). Higher-beta interoperability play.

5. Stellar (XLM)

Clear near-term protocol catalyst: the Bell (Protocol 30) mainnet validator vote is scheduled for November 23 (after a successful testnet vote). The upgrade package includes improvements around smart/muxed contract accounts, post-quantum signature verification support for contracts, and ledger timing refinements. These are operational and longer-term security-oriented changes that could draw attention around the vote date.

Quick context on the tape: The market is in a risk-off/extreme-fear phase with Bitcoin dominance elevated and many alts weaker. Focus on liquidity, risk management, and catalysts rather than chasing recent weekly gainers (many of which are thinner names). Token unlocks (ZRO and others), protocol votes/upgrades (XLM, and later ones like INJ), residual conference effects (SOL), and any macro or ETF flow shifts remain the main near-term variables.
